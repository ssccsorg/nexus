# Kwai Tsing: the midnight drop
#
# One night shift at the Kwai Tsing container terminals, 00:00 to 00:40, and six cases in it. A GBA
# drone lifts a pallet for a European buyer and loses its carrier at 00:10. A port forklift crosses the
# channel it has to descend into at 00:12. Both quay cranes stop for the wind at 00:06. A reefer box
# leaves its window while the straddle is between bays, the north gate refuses a broken seal, and the
# vessel's berth window closes before the pallet ever reaches it.
#
# The drone's own memory keeps writing after the link is gone, which is the whole of the case the
# buyer calls a defect: the record that names the forklift was never on the cloud the link carried.
#
# Every time below is a second of the run, and the project's clock names the scenes they fall in.
