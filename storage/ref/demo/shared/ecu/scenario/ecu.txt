# Brake ECU Bench
#
# An ABS controller writes on bare metal: no operating system, no database, 128 KB of flash and
# 20 KB of RAM. Three weeks later an auditor reads what it wrote, which is the only account of that
# second that exists.
#
# The vehicle writes here and nothing declares anything: four wheels record their pulses, two brake
# controllers their pressure, two sensors the steering angle and the yaw rate, and the auditor's
# console keeps what it read. This is the shape a device that only observes makes, so this scenario
# draws no chain, and the app says so by drawing none.
#

seed 3

# One record per 10 ms in the field, and one a second here: what is kept is the shape, not the rate.
tick 0
write 6 32 wheel-front-left/abs_pulse 1
write 6 32 wheel-front-right/abs_pulse 1
write 6 32 wheel-rear-left/abs_pulse 1
write 6 32 wheel-rear-right/abs_pulse 1
count 24

write 6 24 brake-controller-1/pressure 1
write 6 24 brake-controller-2/pressure 1
count 36

write 6 24 steering-sensor-1/angle 1
write 6 24 yaw-sensor-2/rate 1
count 48

tick 1815000
fact pressure-before 48 brake-controller-1/pressure
fact wheel-lock 48 wheel-front-left/speed
count 50

# What bounded the judgement: the reaction the driver had, and the one the system did.
hint reaction-bounds brake=0.30s,abs=0.30s

flush

# Three weeks later, the auditor's own console keeps what it read, and the read itself is one pass
# over the record's own time axis.
tick 1814400
fact reviewed 48 audit-console-1/review
count 51

query 0 3629440 * 51
