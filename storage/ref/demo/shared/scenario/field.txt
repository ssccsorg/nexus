# Block 7 Field
#
# A soil sensor in a rice field runs on a solar cell the size of a coin: no operating system, no
# database, 32 KB of flash, and it has never been on a network.
#
# The taker here is a person. A device declares the work and the farmer takes it, which is the same
# chain with a pair of hands at the other end, and the app draws it the same way. Four sensors watch
# the field, three pumps record each run they make and a weather station records what the sky did,
# so the salinity the field is judged on has more than one account beside it.
#
#   demo-sim apps/demo-sim/scenario/field.txt /tmp/field-volume --reset

seed 13

# One reading an hour: moisture and salinity, from every sensor in the block.
tick 0
write 6 24 soil-sensor-5/reading 3600
write 6 24 soil-sensor-6/reading 3600
write 6 24 soil-sensor-7/reading 3600
write 6 24 soil-sensor-8/reading 3600
count 24

# The pumps record each run they make, which is the other half of what the sensors are watching.
write 4 24 irrigation-pump-1/run 3600
write 4 24 irrigation-pump-2/run 3600
write 4 24 irrigation-pump-3/run 3600
count 36

write 6 24 weather-station-1/reading 3600
count 42

tick 3628800
fact salinity-up 48 soil-sensor-7/salinity
fact moisture-now 48 soil-sensor-7/moisture
count 44

# What bounded the judgement: the run the pump is allowed, and the salinity it may not pass.
hint pump-bounds run=20min,salinity=0.3pct

# The work the sensor declares, from the reading it found it in, and the person who takes it.
intent shorten-pump salinity-up reduce-pump-to-15min
claim shorten-pump farmer-2
fact pump-for-15min 48 soil-sensor-7/pump
count 45
conclude shorten-pump pump-shortened
count 46

flush

# The next morning the budget is met, and the season is still there.
tick 86400
fact budget-met 48 soil-sensor-7/moisture
count 47

# Two more pieces of work over the same record, and nobody free to take either: the farmer is working
# and the block is left with a queue, which is the reading a space of one piece of work cannot give.
intent watch-salinity moisture-now keep-the-block-drained
intent log-season budget-met write-the-season-notes

query 0 3837600 * 47
